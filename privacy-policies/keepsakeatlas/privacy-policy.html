At a glance: KeepsakeAtlas is designed to work locally on your device. We do not operate a server that receives your collection photos, notes, settings, or backups. The App does not use accounts, advertising, tracking, analytics, cloud sync, push notifications, or third-party crash-reporting services. Camera, photo-library, and location access are optional and are used only for features you choose to use. Membership subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store.

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how Haichao Zhang ("we," "us," or "our") handles information when you use the KeepsakeAtlas iOS application (the "App"), contact us for support, or visit this Privacy Policy page.

This Policy describes the version of the App identified above. If we later introduce features such as online accounts, cloud sync, remote analytics, or notifications, we will update this Policy before those features begin processing information.

2. Information processed locally on your device

2.1 Your collection content

The App stores the following information in its local app sandbox:

  • folders you create, including their names, display themes, and visual skins;
  • collectible records, including a photo or image, a title, notes, rarity, price and currency, the collection date and time, an optional location (city name and/or coordinates), and the order in which items appear in a folder; and
  • the arrangement of your photo wall.

Photos are stored as local files inside the App's sandbox, and the remaining metadata is stored in a local on-device database. All of this processing happens on your device. We do not receive your collection content.

Because titles and notes are free-form, please avoid entering sensitive information that you do not want stored on your device.

2.2 Settings and app state

The App also stores locally:

  • accessibility preferences such as reduced-motion and haptic-feedback settings; and
  • a locally stored indication of whether a membership is active, used to unlock membership features.

2.3 Device and Apple backups

The App does not transmit your collection content or settings to us or to our servers. Depending on your device and backup settings, however, the App's local data may be included in a device, Finder/iTunes, or iCloud backup managed by you or Apple. We cannot access or control those backups. Apple's handling of backup data is governed by Apple's terms and privacy policy.

3. Camera access

With your permission, when you choose to photograph a collectible, the App uses the camera to take photos. Photos you take are saved into the App's local sandbox as part of your collection. They are not uploaded to us or to any server we operate.

Camera access is optional. If you deny or later revoke permission, you can still use the App and add photos from your photo library instead. You can manage camera permission in iOS Settings.

4. Location access

With your permission, when you photograph a collectible the App may use your current location to record where the photo was taken, including a city name and coordinates. The App may also read location data already embedded in photos you import, if the photo contains it.

The city-name lookup and location search features use Apple's system services (Core Location geocoding and Apple Maps). Those requests are handled by Apple under the Apple Privacy Policy; the App does not send your location to us.

Location access is optional and is used only while you use the App. If you deny or later revoke permission, you can still add and organize collectibles without location information. You can manage location permission in iOS Settings.

5. Photo library access

With your permission, the system photo picker lets you select photos to add to your collection. The App copies only the images you select into its local sandbox. It does not automatically access or read your entire photo library, and it does not upload selected photos to us.

Photo-library access is optional. You can revoke or restrict access at any time in iOS Settings.

6. Subscriptions and purchases

KeepsakeAtlas offers a membership through in-app purchases. Purchases, payment, and billing are processed by Apple through StoreKit and the App Store. The App receives only the entitlement status of your membership (for example, whether a membership is active) so it can unlock the corresponding features, and it stores that status locally. We do not receive or store your payment card or other financial details.

Apple may provide us with aggregated App Store statistics, which we use only to understand App distribution and maintain the service.

7. Export and backup

You can export your collection to Excel (.xlsx), PDF, long images, or a KeepsakeAtlas backup (.collect). Exports are generated locally on your device and presented through the iOS share sheet, so you choose where to save, send, or share them. A backup file contains the collection data you choose to include, such as photos, titles, notes, and location information. When you restore a backup, the App reads the file you select and imports the data locally.

We do not receive your exports or backup files.

8. Information the App does not request or transmit to us

The App does not request or automatically transmit to us:

  • names, email addresses, phone numbers, or account credentials;
  • your photo library (only photos you explicitly select are imported);
  • contacts, calendars, health data, or microphone recordings;
  • payment or purchase information;
  • advertising identifiers or other identifiers for tracking;
  • product-interaction analytics or behavioral profiles;
  • crash or performance data through a third-party SDK; or
  • cloud copies of your collection content.

We do not use the App to sell personal information, share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising, serve targeted advertising, or track you across apps or websites owned by other companies.

9. Information you choose to send us

If you email us, we receive the information you choose to provide, which may include your email address, display name, message, attachments, and basic email metadata such as the date and subject. We use this information only to respond to your request, investigate the issue you report, maintain and improve the App's reliability, protect the App and our users, or comply with law. Please do not send sensitive personal information that is unrelated to your request.

Our support address is provided through Microsoft Outlook. Microsoft may process email data on our behalf or as an independent service provider under the Microsoft Privacy Statement.

Location lookups and map search use Apple system services, and membership purchases use StoreKit; each of these is handled by Apple under Apple's terms and privacy policy. If Apple makes aggregated App Store information or user-authorized diagnostic reports available to us, we use them only to understand App distribution and maintain reliability. These services do not give us access to your collection content.

14. Retention and deletion

  • Collection content: retained locally until you delete individual items or folders, restore a backup, or delete the App.
  • Settings and membership status: retained locally until you change them or delete the App.
  • Exports and backups: files you create are stored wherever you choose to save them and are under your control.
  • Support email: retained only for the shortest period reasonably necessary to respond to the request, maintain security, resolve a dispute, or comply with law, and then deleted or irreversibly anonymized. Backup copies may remain until they are overwritten in the ordinary course by the email provider.

Deleting the App removes its active local sandbox from that device. Copies may remain in device or iCloud backups controlled by you or Apple until those backups are deleted or expire under the applicable backup settings. You can manage backups through your device or Apple account.

  • You can delete individual collectibles or folders within the App.
  • You can manage or delete the exports and backups you create.
  • You can delete the App to remove its active local data from the device, subject to backups described above.

16. Children's privacy

KeepsakeAtlas is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information through the App from children, including children under 14 in China, under 13 in the United States, or under the applicable minimum age in another jurisdiction. If you believe a child has sent personal information to our support email, please contact us so we can delete it.

17. Security

We minimize risk by keeping App data within the iOS app sandbox and by not operating a server that receives your collection content.
