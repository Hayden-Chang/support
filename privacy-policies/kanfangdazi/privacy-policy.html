隐私政策 / Privacy Policy

1. 范围与联系

本政策适用于看房搭子 iOS 无需登录的本机记录版本，不适用于微信小程序。运营者：张海超（Haichao Zhang），中国。联系邮箱：shenshuoyouguang@outlook.com。更新日期：2026 年 10 月 30 日；随该版本发布生效。

1. Scope and contact

This policy covers the account-free, on-device iOS edition of Kanfangdazi, not the WeChat mini program. Operator: Haichao Zhang, China. Contact: shenshuoyouguang@outlook.com. Updated October 30, 2026; effective when this edition is released.

2. 本机记录与备份

房源名称、地址、清单答案、问题、备注、照片、视频、通勤结果和设置保存在本机。核心记录不要求注册或登录，不自动上传为云端备份。导出 ZIP 或分享时，所选记录及媒体会交给你选择的文件位置或接收方，请先检查住址、联系方式及他人信息。iOS 或你选择的备份服务可能按其设置备份 App 数据。

2. Local records and backups

Property details, addresses, checklist answers, issues, notes, photos, videos, commute results and settings stay on your device. Core records require no registration or sign-in and are not automatically uploaded as cloud backups. Export and sharing send selected records and media to your chosen destination. Review personal information first. iOS or a backup service you choose may back up app data according to your settings.

3. 权限与可选联网工具

相机用于主动拍摄看房照片或视频，麦克风用于视频录音；系统照片和文件选择器读取你选中的内容。定位仅在你主动使用定位时用于选择房源或上班地点。权限可在 iOS 设置中撤回。地图搜索和显示使用 Apple 地图；路线查询还可能使用天地图，发送地点、坐标或起终点。确认测速后，App 向 Apple 测速端点请求测试数据，服务方会接触连接 IP 等网络信息。拒绝权限或这些工具不可用时，已有本机记录仍可查看和编辑。

3. Permissions and optional online tools

Camera and microphone access capture photos or videos at your request. System photo and file pickers provide selected items. Location helps choose a property or workplace when requested; revoke permissions in iOS Settings. Map search and display use Apple Maps. Routes may also use Tianditu and transmit places, coordinates or endpoints. Confirmed speed tests request data from an Apple endpoint, which receives connection information such as your IP. Existing local records remain usable when permissions are denied or online tools are unavailable.

4. 扩容购买

扩容通过 Apple App 内购买处理，需要联网，但不要求创建看房搭子账号。Apple 处理付款，我们不获取银行卡信息。购买及恢复会处理产品、交易标识、交易验证结果与必要的随机购买标识，用于确认容量、避免重复到账和处理退款。已确认容量保存在本机，离线可继续使用；购买、恢复和重新核验需要联网。购买记录不等于房源或媒体备份，换机前仍需导出。具体商品和恢复条件以已发布版本的购买页面为准。

4. Capacity purchases

Apple handles in-app payments; no Kanfangdazi account is required and we do not receive payment-card details. Purchase and restore process product and transaction identifiers, verification results and necessary random purchase identifiers to verify capacity, prevent duplicate grants and handle refunds. Confirmed capacity is cached for offline use. Purchase, restore and revalidation need a connection. Purchases do not back up records or media. Check the released app for available products and restore conditions.

5. 反馈与支持

仅在点击提交时，App 将你填写的操作感受、检查项评价、功能评价、自由文本和随机请求编号发送至 api.keeline.xyz，用于支持和避免重复提交，不自动附带房源存档或媒体。接口使用连接 IP 进行短时防滥用限制。服务器保存反馈正文、请求编号和时间，目前没有自动到期删除机制。请勿填写不必要的敏感信息。如需查询或删除反馈，请通过联系邮箱提供提交时间和内容线索，我们核实后处理。邮件支持会收到你主动发送的邮件地址和内容。

5. Feedback and support

Only when you submit does the app send usability, checklist and functionality ratings, free text and a random request identifier to api.keeline.xyz for support and duplicate prevention. It does not automatically attach property archives or media. The endpoint uses connection IP addresses for short-term abuse limits. Feedback text, identifiers and times have no automatic expiry. Avoid unnecessary sensitive details. Email the submission time and content details to request access or deletion. Email support receives the address and content you send.

6. 第三方、保存与选择

可选联网功能涉及 Apple、天地图及我们的服务，它们处理请求所需的数据。Apple 隐私说明：https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/ ；天地图：https://www.tianditu.gov.cn/ 。App 不使用广告 SDK、不进行跨 App 跟踪、不出售个人信息。ZIPFoundation 仅用于本机 ZIP 处理。房源及媒体保留至你删除或系统清理；导出副本由你及接收方管理。移除登录入口不会主动删除旧版本本机记录。你可不使用联网工具或购买、撤回权限、导出记录，并在归档房源中永久删除房源及关联媒体。删除 App 或设备故障可能丢失未备份数据。旧账号数据及其他隐私请求可联系邮箱。本版没有新的 App 账号注册或登录；实质变化将更新本页并按适用要求通知。

6. Third parties, retention and choices

Optional services involve Apple, Tianditu and our service. See https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/ and https://www.tianditu.gov.cn/ . The app has no advertising SDK, cross-app tracking or sale of personal information. ZIPFoundation processes local ZIP files. Records remain until you delete them or the system clears storage; exported copies are controlled by you and recipients. Removing sign-in does not itself delete legacy local records. You can avoid online tools and purchases, revoke access, export records, and permanently delete archived properties and their media. App removal or device failure can lose unbacked-up data. Email for legacy account-data or privacy requests. This edition offers no new app registration or sign-in. Material changes will update this page and be communicated as applicable.
