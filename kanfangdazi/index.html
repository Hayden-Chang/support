FAQ

记录会自动备份吗？ / Are records backed up?

App 不自动提供云端房源备份。请在“我的 → 导出房源”保存含媒体的 ZIP；换机前导出，新机导入后核对。删除 App 可能丢失未备份数据。

No automatic cloud record backup is provided. Use My → Export properties to save a ZIP with media, then import and verify it on your new device. Removing the app can lose unbacked-up records.

不登录也能扩容吗？ / Can I buy without sign-in?

本机记录版无需 App 账号。Apple 处理扩容，购买与恢复需要联网，可能要求系统确认 Apple 账户。恢复容量不恢复房源或媒体，商品状态和价格以购买页为准。

No app account is required. Apple handles purchases; purchase and restore need a connection and may request Apple Account authorization. Restoring capacity does not restore records or media. Check the purchase screen for availability and price.

如何删除或撤回权限？ / How can I delete data?

先归档，再到归档房源永久删除，关联媒体一并删除。导出副本需另行删除。权限可在 iOS 设置中撤回；反馈删除及旧账号数据请求请联系邮箱。

Archive, then permanently delete a property in Archived properties to remove its media too. Delete exports separately. Revoke permissions in iOS Settings; email for feedback deletion or legacy account-data requests.

地图、测速不可用怎么办？ / What if online tools fail?

检查网络及所需权限后重试，也可继续手动填写记录。工具暂不可用不会删除已有房源。

Check your connection and permissions, retry, or continue entering records manually. Unavailable tools do not delete properties.
